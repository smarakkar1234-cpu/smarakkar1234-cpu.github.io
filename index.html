Selected work

Three problems, three systems

Each one framed the way I'd walk a client through it: problem, approach, projected value.

Field Service

Triage Copilot for Field Service Dispatch

Problem: Manual ticket triage took 30–40 minutes per case and frequently misrouted to the wrong technician skill set.

Approach: Retrieval-grounded classifier over historical resolutions and skill profiles, with low-confidence cases routed to a human dispatcher instead of auto-assigned.

~25–30% fewer misrouted tickets (modeled)

Finance Ops

Reporting Copilot for Month-End Close

Problem: Analysts spent 60%+ of their time manually compiling and reconciling KPI reports before any real analysis began.

Approach: Natural-language query layer over the existing warehouse, drafting narrative commentary from validated outputs only, with mandatory human sign-off.

~50% faster report turnaround

Knowledge Search

Citation-Grounded Enterprise Research Assistant

Problem: Knowledge workers lost hours searching fragmented internal documentation for recurring research questions.

Approach: RAG pipeline with document-aware chunking, re-ranking, and an evaluation harness benchmarking accuracy before every pipeline change.

90%+ contextual accuracy on eval set
